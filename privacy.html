Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

HookOS ("HookOS", "we", "us") builds tools that turn an idea into a short-form video blueprint. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it — including your rights as a Data Principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act").

1. Data we collect

  • Account data — if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google. We never receive or store your Google password.
  • Content you submit — the topics and frameworks you enter into the generator, and the blueprints produced from them.
  • Usage data — basic request logs (IP address, timestamps, endpoints called) kept for security and abuse prevention.
  • Early access signups — the email address and optional feedback you submit through the Early Access form.

2. Why we process it (purpose limitation)

We only use your data for the purpose you gave it to us: to authenticate you, generate and save your blueprints, respond to support requests, secure the service against abuse, and — if you opt in — contact you about early access. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your data to train third-party AI models beyond the request needed to generate your blueprint.

3. Legal basis and consent

Where the DPDP Act applies, we process your personal data on the basis of your consent, given when you sign in, submit a form, or otherwise use HookOS. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see our Data Deletion page); withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.

4. Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, and as good practice for all users regardless of location, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct or update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your data once it's no longer needed for the purpose collected (see Data Deletion).
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • Register a grievance if you believe we've mishandled your data (see Section 8 below).

5. Data retention

We keep account data for as long as your account is active, and blueprint/history data until you delete it or close your account. Request logs are retained for a limited period for security purposes and then automatically purged. Early access signups are retained until you ask us to remove them.

6. Data sharing and processors

We share data only with the processors needed to run HookOS: our hosting provider (Render), our database provider (Neon/PostgreSQL), and Google (for authentication and, where applicable, the Gemini API used to generate blueprint content). We do not share your data with advertisers.

7. Cross-border data transfer

HookOS's infrastructure may process data outside India. Where required by the DPDP Act, we rely on permitted transfer mechanisms and only work with processors that meet an appropriate standard of data protection.

8. Grievance redressal

If you have a concern about how your data is handled, contact our Grievance Officer at privacy@hookos.app. We aim to acknowledge grievances within a reasonable timeframe and resolve them promptly, consistent with the DPDP Act.

9. Cookies

HookOS uses only strictly necessary cookies (session and security). See our Cookie Policy for details.

10. Children's data

HookOS is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone below the age required to consent to data processing under applicable law.

11. Changes to this policy

We'll update the "Last updated" date above whenever this policy changes materially, and where required, notify you directly.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@hookos.app
