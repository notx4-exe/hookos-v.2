Data Deletion

Last updated: January 2026

You can ask us to erase your HookOS account and the personal data associated with it at any time — this is your right as a Data Principal under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and something we offer to every user regardless of location.

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (name, email, Google account link, avatar).
  • Your saved blueprint history.
  • Your early access signup, if you made one with the same email.

Anonymized request logs kept for security purposes are not tied to your account and age out automatically; they are not part of this request.

How to request deletion

Email privacy@hookos.app from the address associated with your account, with the subject line "Data Deletion Request." We'll verify the request and confirm once it's complete, typically within a reasonable timeframe and no later than required by applicable law.

Self-service deletion

A one-click "Delete my account" option in your profile dashboard is planned for a future release. Until then, the email request above is the fastest way to reach us.
