Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026

HookOS uses a small number of cookies. All of them are strictly necessary — none are used for advertising or third-party tracking.

CookiePurposeDuration
hookos_session Keeps you signed in (httpOnly, not readable by page scripts). 7 days
hookos_oauth_session Short-lived state used only during the Google sign-in handshake. 10 minutes
hookos_csrf Security token that confirms requests originate from HookOS itself. 2 hours

Local storage

We also use your browser's local storage (not a cookie) to remember your cookie-consent choice and, in one place, cached preferences. This data never leaves your device.

Your choices

Because our cookies are strictly necessary for sign-in and security, declining the cookie banner won't block you from using the generator anonymously — but signing in with Google will still set the session cookie described above, since it's required for the sign-in itself to work.
